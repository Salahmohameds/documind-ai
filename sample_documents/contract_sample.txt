SERVICE AGREEMENT

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Company A
("Provider") and Company B ("Client"), collectively the "Parties."

1. TERM
This Agreement shall commence on 2026-01-01 and shall remain in effect
until 2026-12-31, unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 5.

2. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Provider shall deliver cloud architecture consulting, document intelligence
platform development, and ongoing infrastructure support as described in
Exhibit A.

[PAGE 2]
3. FEES AND PAYMENT TERMS
Client shall pay Provider according to the fee schedule in Exhibit B.
Payment is due within 60 days of receipt of a valid invoice. Late payments
shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month.

4. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms
unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 30
days prior to the end of the then-current term.

[PAGE 3]
5. TERMINATION
Either Party may terminate this Agreement for cause upon 15 days written
notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to
cure such breach within the notice period. Client may terminate for
convenience with 30 days written notice.

6. LIABILITY
Provider's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total
fees paid by Client in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim. Neither
Party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

[PAGE 4]
7. CONFIDENTIALITY
Each Party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of the other Party's
proprietary information disclosed during the term of this Agreement, and
for a period of two (2) years following termination.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the applicable jurisdiction
as specified in Exhibit C, without regard to conflict of law principles.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Parties have executed this Agreement as of the
date first written above.
